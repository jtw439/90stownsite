WELCOME TO 90'S TOWN
====================

90's TOWN is a serialized mystery-thriller that asks:

  What if you could bring the past back to life?
  And what if the past wasn't what it seemed to be?

It's a corporate-owned town where the late 1990s
are recreated in every detail. People live here.
People work here. Period accuracy is strictly
enforced.

  No iPhones.
  No broadband.
  No problem.
